folding out now

27 November 2026

Bandcamp buy Subvert.fm buy

This is another track where I could have kept working on it for months, but needed to decide ‘enough is enough’ to get it out for the deadline!

The initial lyric idea was expanded from the demo, which just had a few verse lines and no chorus. The first two lines paired with the generally moody vibe of the track lead me down a darker path into a narrative about someone who isn’t able to accept that a relationship is over.

I’m really enjoying the sound of processed acoustic guitar with my electronic drums - a recurring motif that keeps appearing in my tracks this year. I combined this with a more vintage style mastering, rolling off the highs and sub and reducing the stereo sparkles a little for a more ‘boxed in’ cassette style feeling.

Like many artists, finding ways to create social content without taking too much time away from actually making art has been a constant struggle for me. With this track, I’ve enjoyed making reels using a mini reels maker app I made with Claude, which I use to combine my own video, text and audio to post on social media. This is proving to be a way that I can create social content that makes my music more accessible, relatively quickly, while retaining complete creative control.

My distributor has strict deadlines for holiday-season track releases, which do not play well with my personal release schedule for this year. So, these final four tracks in my challenge will be Bandcamp exclusives - only available on streaming next year when the full album is released.

3 more to go!

I’m posting my progress on Instagram so follow on there for behind the scenes.

Thanks for supporting my work :)
